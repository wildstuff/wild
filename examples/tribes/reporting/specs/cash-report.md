# Cash Report

## Purpose
The weekly cash report: an evaluation over the domain's cash position —
inflows vs. outflows, receivables aging, and the coverage outlook —
published as a document other areas of the `finanzen` domain (and the
operator) can consume.

## Requirements

### Requirement: weekly cash report
The system MUST publish a weekly cash report document summarising the
domain's cash position.
