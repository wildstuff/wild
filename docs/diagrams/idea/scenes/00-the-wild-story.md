# Hero scene — The Wild story, in one picture

## Prompt

**The opening image.** *Three acts on warm paper, left to right along a
thin timeline running from "day one" to "and every day after — it keeps
growing", with a cold counter-strip beneath. Act 1: two piles that never
met — a folder of paperwork noted "the contract lives here" and an
envelope noted "the bill arrives here" — beside a speech bubble saying
"Watch my folder and my mailbox — due dates matter." and a small
friendly green figure labelled Elder listening — captioned "You hand
the topic over. One sentence, in your language — Elder raises a Tribe
for it." Act 2: at the top, the join — a small folder and a small
envelope with two green arrows converging on one rounded node labelled
Partner, noted "the mailed bill finds the folder's contract", with a
small green flag chip beneath it reading "due date flagged" — the join
already paying off; beneath it an anvil with orange sparks — "the tools it was missing" — and a
phone with a simple list — "apps for your people" — captioned "It joins
what belongs together — then it builds on that." Act 3: a bronze shelf
holding three finished things — a graph chip, a tool chip labelled
"parser" with a fresh-forged spark, a phone — under a solid green
banner reading "yours — all of it", captioned "Everything it builds
stays. There from day one, richer every day — on your machine, yours."
Below the timeline, a single cool blue-grey strip: "The other way —
point a model at both piles, every time you ask: every question
re-reads your files, every answer evaporates the moment it's given,"
ending in a crumpled receipt and the words "nothing kept — just the
bill." Bottom line, hand-lettered: "AI that builds, not burns — every
answer adds to something you own; prompting leaves nothing behind."*
