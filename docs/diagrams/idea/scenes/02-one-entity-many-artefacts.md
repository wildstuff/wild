# Hero scene — One entity, many artefacts

## Prompt

**The accumulation image.** *Warm paper, two columns. Down the left, under
a small heading WHAT ARRIVED, four artefact cards of visibly different
kinds, each with its own icon, filename, description and arrival date: a
scanned contract PDF from March, an invoice PDF from April, an e-mail from
the provider mid-April, and a bank-export CSV at the end of April. Green
arrows curve from all four into one large green card on the right, headed
ONE ENTITY, whose title pill reads "Partner · Stadtwerke Nord". Inside the
card is a three-column table — field, value, came from — with five rows:
name, customer no., notice period, billing address, IBAN. Each row's third
column is a rounded chip naming the artefact the value came from, two of
them down to the page: "contract-2019.pdf · p.1" and "contract-2019.pdf ·
p.4". A note at the foot of the card reads "no artefact was consumed — each
field still points at the page it came from". The artefact cards on the
left are drawn intact and undimmed: nothing was swallowed. Bottom line,
hand-lettered: "The entity is not a copy of one document. It is what all of
them, together, say about one thing."*
