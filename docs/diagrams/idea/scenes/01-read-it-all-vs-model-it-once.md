# Hero scene — Read it all, every time · vs · Model it once

## Prompt

**The two ways image.** *A side-by-side comparison on warm paper, three
aligned bands so the eye compares row by row. Left panel, cool blue-grey,
titled "read it all, every time": four labelled source cards — folder,
mailbox, ERP, web — with arrows funnelling down into a single large box
labelled "context window", stuffed with grey fragment lines; caption
"every source, re-read — every time". Right panel, living green, titled
"model it once": the same four sources, but their arrows are dashed and
converge once into a small connected entity graph labelled Atlas, with a
separate question mark bubble arrowing into the graph; caption "one model,
asked again and again". Second band, "the answer": left a speech bubble
saying "Probably three." with nothing under it; right a speech bubble
saying "Three." with two source chips beneath it, contract.pdf and
bill-04.eml. Third band, "rights & time": left a dashed grey padlock
labelled "nothing to attach a grant to" and a dashed clock labelled "each
run discarded"; right a solid green padlock on a field chip reading
"amount · customer_visible", and a bronze timeline with a gold scrub handle
labelled "as of March". Bottom line, hand-lettered: "The model builds the
map once, instead of walking the terrain again on every question."*
