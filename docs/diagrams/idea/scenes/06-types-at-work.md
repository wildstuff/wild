# Hero scene — How the types play together

## Prompt

**The domain-at-work image.** *Warm paper, a small graph of four green type
cards joined by named relations. Partner sits at the top — its fields noted
underneath as "name · customer no. · IBAN", and one white chip inside the
card reading "mark as checked". Two edges labelled "has many" run down from
it: left to Contract ("notice period · renews on"), right to Invoice
("amount · due date"). A horizontal edge labelled "billed under" joins
Contract to Invoice, and one labelled "settled by" drops from Invoice to
Payment ("amount · value date") at the lower right.

Each card carries its effects as chips, and the chips are colour-split by
risk: white-and-green for what the Tribe may do on its own — "send
reminder", "match to invoice", "mark as checked" — and amber with a small
padlock for what waits on a person: "cancel contract" on Contract, "write
off" on Invoice. A legend card at the lower left repeats the split in
words: "the locked ones wait for you — anything that moves money or can't
be undone. The rest the Tribe does on its own, with an audit trail."

Across the foot, a wide green band shows one question being walked rather
than guessed: the question "which contracts renew in the next sixty days?"
in italic, and beneath it "walked as Partner › Contract · renews on — three
of them, each naming the page it was read from", with a quiet note at the
right edge: "no model was asked to guess".*
