# Hero scene — Behind one record: the ontology

## Prompt

**The zoom-out image.** *Warm paper, three panels left to right, joined by
two short grey arrows so the picture reads as stepping back twice. Panel
one, headed ONE RECORD: a single record card for Stadtwerke Nord with three
field rows — name, customer no., notice period — and the note "one of 42
records"; its foot line reads "what you see". Arrow labelled "instance of".
Panel two, headed ITS TYPE — declared once, drawn with the heaviest border
of the three because it is the point of the picture: a solid green header
bar reading "Type · Partner", then three labelled compartments — FIELDS
(name, customer no., notice period, IBAN — each with its kind, the IBAN
noted as checking itself), RELATIONS (has many Contract, has many Invoice),
and EFFECTS (a green "mark as checked" chip and an orange "switch tariff"
chip carrying a padlock). Arrow labelled "one of". Panel three, headed THE
MODEL: a small graph of four connected type nodes — Partner highlighted
solid green at the top, with Contract, Invoice and Payment around it —
under the note "and the rest of your domain"; its foot line reads "what
everything is derived from". Two closing lines run across the bottom:
"Every answer, every app, every worker reads this one declaration — nobody
re-invents what a Partner is." and, in italic, "Records come and go. The
ontology is what stays."*
