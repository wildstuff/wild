# Hero scene — The cast

## Prompt

**The character sheet.** *Six cards on warm paper, three across and two
down. Each card carries an emblem on the left, a name in large hand-lettered
type, a small rounded scope chip under the name, four short lines of role
description, and one italic line at the foot that states what the character
is for. Elder — an indigo stone archway with a small gold lamp above it,
"one per install", the gatekeeper who onboards new Tribes and routes you
between them; foot line "you talk to Elder — never to the machinery". Atlas
— three green nodes connected into a triangle, resting on a bronze ground
line, "one per Tribe", the ground everything stands on; foot line "throw
away every prompt — this survives". Chief — a crowned charter inside a
dashed circle with an arrow sweeping around it, "one per Tribe", reads the
charter and runs the cycles; foot line "keeps working between your
messages". Worker — three small green figures side by side, "many per
Tribe", the specialists doing the actual work; foot line "AI-, rule-,
shell-, api- or human-backed". Forge — a grey anvil throwing orange sparks,
"one per Tribe", the smithy that builds a missing tool; foot line "this is
what makes a Tribe generative". App — a phone showing a list with a green
check, "many per Tribe", what a Tribe hands the people around you; foot
line "derived from the ontology, not developed". A single line runs across
the bottom: "One install: one Elder, many Tribes. One Tribe: one Chief, one
Atlas, one Forge — and as many Workers and Apps as it needs."*
