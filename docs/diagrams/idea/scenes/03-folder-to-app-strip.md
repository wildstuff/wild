# Hero scene — From a folder to an app

## Prompt

**The story strip.** *Six panels on warm paper, three across and two down,
each a white card with a green numbered badge in its corner and a short
hand-lettered caption underneath. Panel 1: a folder of paperwork with loose
sheets peeking out and a cursor arrow pointing at it — "You point at a
folder. Invoices, letters, contracts." Panel 2: a small friendly green
figure labelled Elder beside a speech bubble asking three short questions —
"due dates?", "notice periods?", "who may see them?" — captioned "Elder
asks what matters. A few questions — then it raises a Tribe for the topic."
Panel 3: three connected rounded nodes, Partner on top with Contract and
Invoice hanging beneath it — "It learns what your things are. Your things,
and how they belong together — built once." Panel 4: a small folder icon
and a small envelope icon, each with a curved green arrow running down into
one shared node labelled Partner — "Two sources, one thing. Both receipts
kept." Panel 5: an anvil with orange sparks flying off it and a freshly
made chip labelled "parser" beside it marked "✦ new tool" — "It builds the
missing tool. Real code — no model in the loop." Panel 6: a phone showing a simple
list with a green check mark, noted "on their phone" — "Your partner opens
the app. Nobody wrote code." Bottom line, hand-lettered: "Not software you
rent — a system you raised, and everything it built is yours."*
