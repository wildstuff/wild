# 🎨 wild-arc — hero figure, visual language

`wild-arc.svg` is the hand-built outer-layer figure in
[`idea.md` § How it runs](../idea.md#how-it-runs) and the **style
reference for every user-facing figure** (the showcase scenes under
`docs/diagrams/*/scenes/` follow it). The README hero is scene
`00-the-wild-story` from `docs/diagrams/idea/scenes/`. Use this prompt to generate a
higher-fidelity editorial version; keep composition and label
wording identical.

## The visual language (the rules that make it feel native)

- **White canvas, hairline borders** (`#d0d7de`), 16–20 px corner
  radius, one soft drop shadow — never hard outlines or saturated
  fills.
- **System typography** (SF Pro / -apple-system): semibold
  `#1f2328` for the few primary labels, regular `#57606a`
  secondary, tiny letter-spaced `#8c959f` uppercase kickers for
  zone titles.
- **Line icons, not filled blobs** — 1.8 px round-cap strokes
  (folder, envelope, globe, phone, chat bubble).
- **One accent per figure**, used at most three times: Apple blue
  `#007aff` (plus green `#34c759` for the Tribe / success, orange
  `#ff9500` only for the money line). Everything else grayscale.
- **Thin gray arrows** (`#8c959f`, 1.4 px, small open chevron
  heads), dashed + dotted lines for spoken/asking relations.
- **Generous whitespace.** If a label can be cut, cut it.

> 2. Left, kicker YOUR WORLD: three line icons — folder
>    ("Folders — invoices · contracts · scans"), envelope
>    ("Mail — what arrives by itself"), globe ("Web & drives —
>    portals · SharePoint") — with thin arrows converging on one
>    point of the central card.
> 3. Center: a large soft card titled **The model of your world**
>    — *"built once — connected, searchable, every fact with its
>    origin"*. On it three white chips joined by lines: Supplier —
>    Invoice — Contract, with the blue tag *"one entity — fed by
>    folder & mail"*. Below, a thin timeline of dots with one
>    ringed dot and a small pill *"as of March 31"*, caption *"any
>    day, replayable"*. Standing on the card's top edge: three
>    green outline circles (one larger, unlabeled), side note
>    *"your Tribe — it works the topic and builds the tools it's

>    grows is yours, on your machine."*
> No photorealism, no gradients, no 3D, no faces, no logos, no
> text beyond these labels.
